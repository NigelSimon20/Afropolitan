## These are stand-ins, not Afropolitan

Every one of these shows **a different restaurant's room, food and drinks**.
They are here so the design can be presented with real photography instead of
abstract placeholders — nothing more.

Before the site goes live, Afropolitan needs a shoot of their own. A site that
shows food they don't serve and a room that isn't theirs will mislead customers
who arrive expecting it, and it is the one part of this build that still
implies something untrue about the business.

### Screened before use

Every candidate was checked at full resolution for other businesses' branding.
Four were rejected and replaced during selection:

- a hero showing a neon **"B"** and a lit **"BASTA H\_RO"** sign
- an exterior reading **"LE NEMOURS CAFE BAR"**
- a bar with a fully legible **beer list and dollar prices** on a chalkboard
- a bright daytime festival crowd, off-brand for the Occasions band

If you swap any image, check the full-size file for signage, menu boards and
price lists before it ships.
